How a stamp is earned

Four pillars, each 0–10. No source attached, no stamp issued.

01

Transparency

Does the ministry publish its own data, answer RTI requests on time, and respond fully to Parliament questions?

02

Delivery

Are stated scheme targets — roads built, exams held, funds disbursed — actually met, per official or audit data?

03

Responsiveness

When something fails publicly, how fast and how substantively does the ministry act?

04

Fiscal discipline

What do CAG reports and budget-versus-actual data say about how public money was used?

The one rule that overrides everything else iMinistry rates ministries, never people. Every stamp requires a public, checkable source — a CAG report, an RTI reply, a Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha answer, a PIB release, a court order, or a dated news report with a link. Unsourced claims are rejected, not published. See the full rubric in METHODOLOGY.md.
